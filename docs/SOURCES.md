# Research used in Little Tides

Checked November 10, 2026. Behavior and physics parameters remain game approximations.

- [Manitoba provincial Big Whiteshell fisheries assessment](https://www.manitoba.ca/nrnd/fish-wildlife/pubs/lifa/big_whiteshell.pdf): confirms walleye, northern pike, white sucker, and yellow perch in the starting water.
- [Travel Manitoba Master Angler rules and minimum lengths](https://anglers.travelmanitoba.com/master-angler-program/rules/): starter trophy lengths. The game uses its own local achievements and does not submit real awards.
- [Manitoba Red River fishway report](https://content.gov.mb.ca/mit/wm/design_fishway.pdf): researched species for a possible later Manitoba water.
- [Minnesota DNR Red River fishing](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/fisheries/red-river-fishing.html): regional fish and bait context.
- [Wisconsin DNR fishing guidance](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/Fishing/outreach/hookyourcatch.html): worms and small invertebrate baits for panfish.
- [Dogskin Manitoba walleye and pike tackle guidance](https://dogskin.squarespace.com/faq): night crawlers, preserved minnows, jigs, pike spoons, and stronger leaders.
- [Kilbirnie Angling Club](https://klmg.org.uk/pages/kilbirnie-angling-club): future Scotland candidate species.
- [Forth Rivers Trust brown trout](https://forthriverstrust.org/rivers-wildlife/learn/wildlife/fish/brown-trout/): insect and small-fish prey.
- [Visit Southern Norway fishing](https://en.visitsorlandet.com/things-to-do/nature-experiences/fishing-in-southern-norway/): future coastal species candidates.

The game itself does not access these sites or require a network connection.

## Fish appearance review, revision 3

- [Michigan DNR yellow perch](https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/education/michigan-species/fish-species/yellow-perch): yellow sides, about seven vertical bars, separated spiny and soft dorsal fins.
- [Ohio DNR yellow perch](https://ohiodnr.gov/discover-and-learn/animals/fish/yellow-perch): orange lower fins and dark posterior blotch on the first dorsal.
- [Ontario walleye](https://www.ontario.ca/page/walleye): elongated olive/yellow profile, large jaw, separated dorsals, and white lower tail tip.
- [Minnesota DNR northern pike](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/minnaqua/speciesprofile/northern_pike.html): flattened duckbill snout and a soft dorsal near the tail.
- [Missouri Department of Conservation northern pike](https://mdc.mo.gov/discover-nature/field-guide/northern-pike): dorsal and anal fins positioned opposite each other far back, near the forked tail.
- [Manitoba baitfish identification guide](https://www.gov.mb.ca/nrnd/fish-wildlife/pubs/fish_wildlife/baitguide.pdf): white sucker's cylindrical body, blunt head, downward fleshy lips, single soft dorsal, and grey-brown back over a pale belly.

All sprites are original drawings encoded in the game source. Reference facts inform the anatomy; no third-party fish illustration is embedded in the game.
